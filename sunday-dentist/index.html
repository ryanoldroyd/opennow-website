The Honest Truth

Who else is actually open on Sunday?

Not many, and it's worth being clear-eyed about your options:

  • Most dental offices are closed. Standard hours run Monday through Thursday, occasionally a half-day Friday. Sunday is dark for the large majority of practices.
  • Emergency rooms can't fix teeth. An ER can help with a dangerous infection or facial trauma, but they generally don't do dental work — most toothache visits end with painkillers, maybe antibiotics, and a note to see a dentist. That's a long, expensive wait for a problem that's still there.
  • Urgent-care clinics have the same limitation. They're not staffed or equipped to treat the tooth itself.

That gap is the entire reason we open on Sundays. When you call us, you reach a team that can actually diagnose and treat the problem the same day — not just tide you over until Monday.
