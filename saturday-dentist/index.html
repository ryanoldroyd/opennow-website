Full Care on Saturdays

Saturday is a full clinic day, not a triage line

Some offices squeeze in a rare Saturday morning for emergencies only. We don't work that way. Every Saturday is a complete day of care — fully staffed and equipped for the same treatments you'd get midweek:

  • Cleanings, exams & digital X-rays — the checkup that keeps slipping off your to-do list.
  • Fillings, crowns & bridges — tooth-colored restorations, frequently in one visit.
  • Root canals & extractions — comfortable, modern care with sedation available for nervous patients.
  • Cosmetic dentistry — whitening, veneers, and bonding, timed so you're ready for the week.
  • Same-day emergencies — the broken teeth and toothaches that ruin a weekend. See our emergency dentist page.

Why Saturdays Work

The appointment that doesn't cost you a workday

A weekday dental visit usually means requesting time off, rearranging meetings, or missing a class. A Saturday visit costs you none of that — which is exactly why our Saturdays fill with people who'd otherwise keep putting care off:

  • Whole families in one trip. Book back-to-back appointments and knock everyone's checkup out at once.
  • Early-morning slots. Come in at 8am and still have your whole Saturday ahead of you.
  • Shift workers and commuters. A schedule that finally lines up with a Monday–Friday grind.

Because Saturdays are popular, the earlier slots go first — calling ahead is the surest way to get the time you want.

The Honest Truth

Who else is open on Saturday?

Weekend dental care is rarer than people expect. A handful of offices offer occasional Saturday mornings, but most are closed all weekend. And for a genuine emergency, the ER isn't a real substitute — hospitals generally can't treat the tooth itself, so a Saturday ER trip for a toothache usually ends with painkillers and a referral to a dentist. We open on Saturdays precisely so the south valley has somewhere to get the problem actually fixed — same day.

Saturday FAQ

Do you charge extra for Saturday visits?

No. Saturday visits cost exactly the same as a weekday appointment — there's no weekend surcharge. You'll always get a clear estimate before any treatment starts.

Can my whole family be seen the same Saturday?

Yes — Saturdays are ideal for it. Let us know how many people and roughly what each needs when you call, and we'll line up back-to-back appointments so it's one trip instead of several.

Do I need to be an existing patient?

No. New patients are welcome every day we're open. Call ahead so we can reserve enough time and get your new-patient paperwork ready.
