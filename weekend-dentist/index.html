Why Weekends

We flipped the schedule on purpose

Most dental offices keep banker's hours — the exact hours you're at work or getting the kids to school. So good people put off care they know they need, and small problems quietly become big ones. We thought that was backwards.

So we built our entire practice around the times real people are actually free: Friday afternoons and all weekend. This isn't an after-hours side hustle bolted onto a weekday office. It's the whole thing — a fully credentialed, fully equipped dental practice that just happens to be open when you can finally come in. The result is calmer visits, shorter waits, and far fewer people walking in already in pain.

Full Practice, Full Weekend

Everything we do — every Saturday and Sunday

Because we're a complete practice, your weekend appointment can be anything a weekday one would be:

  • Cleanings, exams & digital X-rays — including that long-overdue checkup that never fit your calendar.
  • Fillings, crowns & bridges — tooth-colored restorations, often in a single visit.
  • Root canals & extractions — modern, comfortable techniques, with sedation available.
  • Cosmetic dentistry — whitening, veneers, and bonding to have you camera-ready by Monday.
  • Kids & families — see everyone in one trip instead of pulling children out of school.
  • Same-day emergencies — for the toothaches and broken teeth that don't wait. See our emergency dentist page.

Who We Serve

Built for people with full lives

Weekend hours aren't a gimmick — they're the difference between getting care and skipping it for the folks we see most:

  • Shift workers and healthcare staff who can't take a random Tuesday afternoon off.
  • Busy parents juggling work, school pickups, and a soccer schedule that owns the weekdays.
  • Anyone who's been putting off a cleaning for a year (or three) because the timing never worked.
  • Travelers and weekend visitors who cracked a tooth away from their regular dentist.

No judgment, no lectures — just care that finally fits the way you actually live.
